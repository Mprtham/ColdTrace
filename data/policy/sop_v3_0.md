# ColdTrace Cold-Chain SOP v3.0
effective_date: 2024-06-01
version: 3.0

## Section 1.1 — Temperature Breach: Fresh Perishables
Cargo type: fresh_perishables
Safe range: 0.0°C to 4.0°C
If temperature exceeds 4.0°C, the dispatcher must contact the driver
within 5 minutes. If the breach persists for 30 minutes or more,
escalate immediately to the Tier 2 Logistics Manager and divert the
vehicle to the nearest emergency cold-storage facility.

## Section 1.2 — Temperature Breach: Frozen Goods
Cargo type: frozen
Safe range: -25.0°C to -18.0°C
Any reading above -18.0°C triggers an immediate driver contact.
Escalate to Tier 2 if breach exceeds 30 minutes.

## Section 2.1 — Port Congestion Diversion
If port congestion index exceeds 7.0, divert all active shipments
to the Inland Empire Overflow Depot for cross-docking.
Do not hold freight at the port.

## Section 3.1 — High Risk Escalation
Any shipment classified High Risk with delay probability above 0.65
must be escalated to the Tier 2 Logistics Manager within 10 minutes
of classification.
